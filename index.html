THE LITTLE THINGS BECOME EVERYTHING

Pieces of us.

A few frames, a thousand feelings.

01 / HER

That smile of yours.

Baby, there is something about this picture that makes me stop for a second and just look at you. Maybe it is the way you carry yourself, maybe it is your quiet confidence, or maybe it is simply because it is you. I know a photograph can never capture a whole person, but this one reminds me how many little things I adore about you. I love that you can make an ordinary day feel worth remembering without even trying. When I look at this photo, I don't just see a pretty picture; I see a memory I am lucky to have, and a person I want to keep making memories with. I hope this birthday reminds you to see yourself with the same kindness and admiration that I see you with. You deserve to feel beautiful, appreciated, and loved—not only today, but on all the ordinary days too. ♡

02 / US

My favourite frame.

There are pictures that look good, and then there are pictures that feel like something. This one belongs to the second kind for me. Seeing us together makes me think about how a connection can grow from one unexpected hello into a collection of moments that start to mean the world. I may not remember every little detail of the day this photo was taken, but I know I want to remember the feeling: being beside you, sharing a moment, and having one more memory that is ours. If I could keep a tiny album of the moments that make me smile on a difficult day, this picture would absolutely be in it. I don't need every moment with you to be grand or perfect. Give me the simple ones, the silly ones, the unplanned ones, and the ones where we are just being ourselves. Those are the moments I want more of. Thank you for being part of my favourite frames, Baby. ❤️

03 / ALWAYS

Just being us.

What I like most about memories like this is that they don't need a special explanation. It is just you and me, sharing a little piece of time, and somehow that is enough to make the picture important to me. One day, we might look back at these photos and laugh at our poses, our expressions, or the things we had completely forgotten about. I hope we do. I hope we keep collecting pictures that remind us how much life happened in between the big milestones. I want us to have more random selfies, more silly moments, more conversations that go on longer than planned, and more days where we look at each other and think, “I am glad we have this.” I cannot promise every day will be perfect, but I can promise that I want to keep showing up with care, honesty, and effort. For now, this photo is a small reminder of something I never want to take for granted: the simple happiness of being us. ♡

04 / A LITTLE ADVENTURE

One day, one memory at a time.

This little snapshot reminds me that a day does not have to be carefully planned to become a memory worth keeping. Sometimes all it takes is being together, walking around, noticing the small things, and taking a picture before the moment passes. I want us to have many more days like that: exploring new places, finding our favourite corners of the city, taking photos that are slightly blurry, and laughing about things that probably make sense only to us. I want to remember the journeys as much as the destinations, because the best part is not always where we go—it is who is beside us while we get there. Maybe years from now, we will see this picture again and remember the weather, the conversation, or something funny that happened just before it was taken. Until then, let it be a little promise to ourselves: there are still so many places to see, so many stories to write, and so many ordinary moments that can become extraordinary simply because we share them. ❤️
